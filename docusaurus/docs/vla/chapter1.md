# VLA - Chapter 1: Introduction to Vision-Language Models for Robotics

This chapter introduces the concept of Vision-Language Models (VLAs) and their transformative potential in enabling more intuitive and intelligent human-robot interaction.

## 1.1 What are Vision-Language Models?
- Overview of VLAs and their architecture (e.g., CLIP, ViLT).
- How VLAs bridge the gap between visual perception and natural language understanding.

## 1.2 The Need for VLAs in Robotics
- Challenges in traditional robot programming and control.
- Enabling natural language commands and abstract goal specification.

## 1.3 Key Concepts and Terminology
- Vision encoders, language encoders, and multimodal fusion.
- Grounding, referring expressions, and task planning with language.

## 1.4 Applications of VLAs in Physical AI
- Semantic navigation, object manipulation, and task execution.
- Humanoid robots understanding and responding to complex instructions.
